# 🏠 HomeService App

> **A smart, reliable, and convenient platform for booking professional home services on demand.**

---

## 📖 Overview

**HomeService App** is a modern application designed to seamlessly connect homeowners with verified local service professionals. From plumbing, electrical repairs, and appliance maintenance to home cleaning and painting, this platform empowers users to effortlessly find, book, and pay for trusted services right from their smartphones.

The project aims to simplify everyday household chores and maintenance by delivering a fast, transparent, and hassle-free service booking experience.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 👨‍💼 For Customers
* **Service Discovery:** Search and browse through various categories such as Cleaning, Electrical, Plumbing, AC Repair, and Carpentry.
* **Flexible Booking:** Book an immediate service or schedule an appointment for a preferred date and time.
* **Real-Time Technician Tracking:** Live location tracking to monitor when the service provider will arrive.
* **Multiple Payment Options:** Support for Cash on Delivery, Credit/Debit Cards, and Mobile Banking.
* **In-App Messaging & Calling:** Direct communication with assigned service providers.
* **Ratings & Reviews:** Rate and review completed services to ensure quality control.

### 🛠️ For Service Providers & Technicians
* **Job Dashboard:** View, accept, or reject incoming service requests based on availability.
* **Earnings Tracking:** Real-time tracking of daily, weekly, and monthly earnings.
* **Route Navigation:** Integrated maps for easy navigation to customer locations.
